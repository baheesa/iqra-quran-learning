# 22 - Curriculum Specification

> "The curriculum defines what the learner learns. The AI only helps deliver it."

---

# Purpose

This document defines the complete educational structure of the application.

It is independent of:

- UI
- Database
- AI Provider
- Backend
- Frontend

It describes only the learning journey.

---

# Educational Goal

The learner should gradually become capable of opening the Quran and understanding it directly without relying on translation.

The learner should:

Recognize words.

Recognize patterns.

Connect vocabulary.

Understand meaning naturally.

Read fluently.

---

# Source Material

Primary source:

Muallim-ul-Quran

Books are available only as scanned image PDFs.

Every lesson must be traceable to the original pages.

Rules introduced.

Exercises completed.

---

# AI Assistance

The AI should help only when:

The learner asks.

The learner repeatedly struggles.

A review indicates difficulty.

Otherwise, allow independent recognition.

---

# Recognition Policy

Always encourage recall before explanation.

Examples:

"Have you seen this word before?"

"What lesson introduced this?"

"What similar word do you remember?"

Recognition strengthens long-term understanding.

---

# Grammar Policy

Grammar is not the focus.

Grammar should appear only when:

The curriculum introduces it.

The learner requests clarification.

It genuinely supports understanding.

Otherwise, prioritize vocabulary recognition.

---

# Progress Measurement

Track:

Lessons completed

Vocabulary confidence

Rule confidence

Reading consistency

Review completion

Do not rank learners against others.

---

# Success Indicators

A learner is progressing when:

They recognize more Quranic words.

They rely less on translation.

They ask fewer AI questions.

They read with increasing confidence.

---

# Future Expansion

The curriculum should support:

Additional Muallim editions

Advanced levels

Children's tracks

Teacher mode

Multiple languages

Without changing the core progression.

---

# Final Principle

The curriculum is the foundation of the application.

Technology may evolve.

AI models may improve.

The curriculum remains stable.

Everything else exists to help the learner progress through it.
