# 06 - Reading Engine

> "The Reading Engine is the heart of the application.
> The learner opens the app to read the Quran, not to chat with AI."

---

# Purpose

The Reading Engine provides a calm, distraction-free Quran reading experience while quietly connecting the learner to:

- Muallim-ul-Quran
- Vocabulary
- Rules
- Personal Progress
- AI Teacher

The learner should always feel that they are reading the Quran.

Not using an application.

---

# Core Philosophy

Reading is the primary activity.

Everything else is secondary.

The application should never interrupt the learner unnecessarily.

The Reading Engine exists to help the learner continue reading with increasing confidence.

---

# Primary Objectives

The Reading Engine should allow the learner to:

✓ Read comfortably

✓ Recognize vocabulary

✓ Understand naturally

✓ Continue reading

✓ Ask questions only when needed

The learner should remain focused on the Quran.

---

# Reading Flow

Every reading session follows the same pattern.

Open Quran

↓

Continue from last position

↓

Read naturally

↓

Tap only when necessary

↓

Continue reading

↓

Finish

↓

Reflection

↓

Review generated automatically

No extra steps.

---

# Continue Where You Left Off

Whenever the learner opens the app,

they should immediately see:

Current Juz

↓

Current Surah

↓

Current Ayah

↓

Resume Reading

No searching.

No navigation.

One tap.

---

# Reading Modes

Version 1 should support only one reading mode.

Sequential Reading

Juz 1

↓

Juz 2

↓

...

↓

Juz 30

No random jumping.

No daily verse.

No topic mode.

The learner follows the Quran in order.

---

# Reading Session

Each session should store:

Date

Start Time

End Time

Duration

Juz

Surah

Starting Ayah

Ending Ayah

Words Tapped

Questions Asked

Mistakes

Reflection

Generated Reviews

---

# Session Duration

Recommended duration:

15–20 minutes

The application should gently encourage ending the session after the planned time.

Example:

"You've completed today's study."

Consistency is preferred over long sessions.

---

# Quran Display

The Quran should occupy most of the screen.

Avoid unnecessary UI.

Avoid large buttons.

Avoid distracting colors.

The learner should feel like they are looking at a Mushaf.

---

# Quran Font

Use the Indo-Pak Quran script.

Do not substitute another Arabic font.

Maintain authentic page layout where possible.

Respect traditional typography.

---

# Urdu Interface

The interface language is Urdu.

Buttons

Menus

Settings

Hints

Explanations

Progress

Everything should be in Urdu.

Only Quranic Arabic remains unchanged.

---

# Word Interaction

The learner may tap any Quranic word.

When tapped,

show a small panel.

Do not open a full-screen chat.

The panel should display:

Word

Recognition Status

Confidence

Lesson Introduced

Related Rule

Occurrences

Need Help?

The learner decides whether to ask the AI.

---

# Recognition First

Before revealing anything,

ask:

"Do you recognize this word?"

Possible responses:

Yes

Maybe

No

Recognition should always come before explanation.

---

# AI Assistance

If the learner requests help,

the AI should answer within the context of the current verse.

The AI should not begin unrelated discussions.

Keep the learner inside the reading experience.

---

# Vocabulary Highlighting

Highlighting should be optional.

Possible states:

Known Word

Learning Word

Weak Word

New Word

Colors should remain subtle.

The Quran should remain visually clean.

---

# Rule Indicators

If a word relates to a Muallim rule,

show a small indicator.

Example:

📘 Related Rule

The learner may open it if desired.

Never force interruptions.

---

# Reading Without AI

The learner should be able to read the entire Quran without opening AI once.

AI is optional.

Reading is mandatory.

---

# Reflection

At the end of every session,

ask only one question.

Examples:

What new word did you recognize today?

Which verse became easier?

Which rule helped you today?

Reflection should take less than one minute.

---

# Automatic Review Creation

When reading ends,

the Reading Engine automatically creates review items.

Examples:

Weak words

Forgotten words

Rules needing review

Repeated mistakes

The learner never creates reviews manually.

---

# Progress Tracking

Track reading naturally.

Examples:

Current Juz

Current Surah

Current Ayah

Today's Reading

Total Reading Time

Words Recognized

Vocabulary Growth

Do not overload the learner with statistics.

---

# Bookmarks

Allow unlimited bookmarks.

Each bookmark stores:

Location

Title

Personal Note

Date

Bookmarks are for the learner,

not for the AI.

---

# Personal Notes

The learner may write personal reflections.

Notes belong to the learner.

The AI may reference them later if relevant.

---

# Offline Reading

Reading should work without AI.

Available offline:

Quran

Bookmarks

Vocabulary Database

Rules

Lessons

Progress

Reviews

Only AI reasoning requires an internet connection.

---

# Reading Performance

The Reading Engine should quietly measure:

Average session length

Reading consistency

Average questions per session

Average words recognized

Improvement over time

These metrics are for personalization,

not competition.

---

# Things the Reading Engine Must Never Do

❌ Interrupt reading

❌ Display advertisements

❌ Force quizzes

❌ Force lesson completion

❌ Auto-open AI

❌ Cover the Quran with large dialogs

❌ Encourage unnecessary clicking

The learner should always return to reading quickly.

---

# Integration with Other Engines

The Reading Engine communicates with:

Curriculum Engine

→ Today's lesson

Learner Engine

→ Confidence

Teacher Engine

→ AI guidance

Knowledge Engine

→ Vocabulary

Review Engine

→ Future reviews

The Reading Engine itself remains simple.

Its responsibility is reading.

---

# Success Criteria

The Reading Engine succeeds when:

The learner opens the app.

Reads comfortably.

Asks fewer questions over time.

Recognizes more vocabulary every month.

Needs less AI assistance.

Feels closer to the Quran.

---

# Final Principle

The Reading Engine should disappear.

The learner should feel as if they are simply sitting with the Quran.

Everything else should remain quietly in the background.

If the learner forgets they are using software,

the Reading Engine has succeeded.
