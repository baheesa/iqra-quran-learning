# Learner features manual

How to use the Quran Learning System (web + Iqra offline app).

Purpose: help you **read the Quran**, **recognize words**, and **depend less on translation**.

---

## Daily habit (15–20 minutes)

1. Open **Quran** and read a page calmly.
2. Tap any word you are unsure about — its **Urdu tip** appears on the word.
3. Mark unit words / ayahs / qawaid as you become familiar.
4. Optionally revise **My words**, **Qawaid**, or a short **dua**.

---

## Quran (mushaf)

- Indo-Pak script, page / juz / surah navigation.
- **Tap a word** → short Urdu meaning tip (word-by-word).
- Tip closes when you tap elsewhere.
- Search Arabic or Urdu; open a match on the mushaf with highlight.
- Optional ayah Urdu on demand (translate icon) — recognition first, translation second.
- Bookmarks and last page are remembered.

## Qawaid (rules)

- Short patterns from **Muallim-ul-Quran**, units 1 → 7.
- Each rule: title, brief definition, examples.
- Examples show **Urdu** when available.
- Tap an example word for a **standalone** word tip (not tied to one ayah context).
- Mark rules learned when you recognize them in reading.

## Duas

- Masnoon and **Qur’anic** duas.
- Qur’anic entries show the **dua excerpt** (not the full surrounding story), with **matching Urdu**.
- Tap Arabic words for tips.
- Book icon opens that place in the mushaf.
- Mark Qur’anic duas memorized when ready.

## Unit words & ayahs

- Curriculum vocabulary and practice ayahs by unit.
- Search, mark familiar, open related mushaf page.
- Tap Arabic for word tips (ayah-scoped when a ref exists).

## My words

- Words you tapped while reading (practice list).
- Review meanings you already met in the mushaf.

## Home

- Gentle entry: continue reading, journey ayahs, links into the main areas.

---

## What this app is not

Not a chatbot, not a translation app, not a grammar course, not quizzes or social features.

The Quran stays at the center. The teacher (AI / tips) only **helps recognition**.

---

## Tips for better learning

- Prefer **reading** over collecting tips.
- Use tips to **confirm**, not to replace thinking.
- Return to the same pages until words feel familiar.
- Use Qawaid when a pattern repeats and you want the short rule.
