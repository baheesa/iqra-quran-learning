# 02 - Learning Methodology

> "People do not learn a language by receiving answers.
> They learn by repeatedly recognizing meaning in context."

This document defines how learning takes place inside the application.

It is one of the core documents of the project.

The AI must follow these principles when teaching.

---

# Purpose

The objective is not to teach Arabic grammar.

The objective is not to memorize vocabulary.

The objective is:

Help the learner gradually understand the Quran directly while reading it.

Everything in this application should support this objective.

---

# The Learning Triangle

Learning is based on three components.

                Muallim-ul-Quran
                     │
                     │
                     ▼
               AI Teacher
                     ▲
                     │
                     │
                  Learner
                     │
                     ▼
                  Quran

Every study session connects these four elements.

The AI never teaches independently.

It teaches according to Muallim-ul-Quran while guiding the learner through the Quran.

---

# The Daily Study Cycle

Each session should last approximately 15–20 minutes.

The recommended flow is:

1. Review yesterday

↓

2. Learn today's Muallim lesson

↓

3. Practice today's vocabulary

↓

4. Read today's Quran portion

↓

5. Ask questions

↓

6. Review mistakes

↓

7. Finish

The learner should feel encouraged rather than overwhelmed.

---

# The AI Teaching Cycle

Whenever the learner asks a question, the AI should follow this sequence.

Step 1

Determine what the learner already knows.

↓

Step 2

Search Muallim-ul-Quran.

↓

Step 3

Connect the lesson to the Quran.

↓

Step 4

Ask the learner to think.

↓

Step 5

Provide hints.

↓

Step 6

Provide the explanation.

↓

Step 7

Recommend revision if necessary.

The AI should not skip directly to explanations.

---

# The Learning Order

Learning should occur in the following order.

Recognition

↓

Understanding

↓

Confidence

↓

Automatic Recognition

↓

Natural Reading

Translation exists only when necessary.

---

# Vocabulary Learning

Vocabulary should never be presented as isolated words.

Every word must be connected to:

its lesson

its rule

its examples

its Quran occurrences

its review history

its confidence score

Words are learned through repeated encounters.

---

# Rule Learning

Rules should always be connected to examples.

Never teach abstract rules without showing them inside the Quran.

Every rule page should contain:

Rule

Explanation

Muallim page

Examples

Exercises

Related Quran verses

Revision history

Confidence

---

# Quran Reading

The learner reads continuously.

The AI interrupts only when requested.

Reading flow:

Read

↓

Think

↓

Continue

The Quran remains the focus.

---

# AI Intervention

The AI should intervene only when:

The learner asks.

The learner repeatedly struggles.

The learner forgets a known word.

The learner requests revision.

Otherwise,

allow uninterrupted reading.

---

# Encouraging Recognition

Suppose the learner clicks:

الحمد

The AI should NOT immediately say:

"It means praise."

Instead:

Have you seen this before?

↓

Which lesson introduced it?

↓

Can you remember the meaning?

↓

Try reading the whole verse again.

↓

Then explain if needed.

Recognition develops understanding.

---

# Understanding Before Memorization

The learner is not expected to memorize long vocabulary lists.

Understanding grows naturally through repeated Quran reading.

The application should encourage:

reading

recognition

reflection

revision

rather than memorization.

---

# Mistakes

Mistakes are valuable.

Every mistake becomes future learning.

Whenever a learner struggles:

record it

schedule revision

increase review frequency

connect it to the relevant Muallim lesson

Mistakes should never discourage.

They should guide future lessons.

---

# Confidence

Every word has a confidence score.

Confidence increases through successful recognition.

Confidence decreases after repeated mistakes.

Example

0–20

Unknown

21–40

Learning

41–60

Familiar

61–80

Known

81–100

Mastered

Confidence changes continuously.

---

# Revision Philosophy

Revision is continuous.

Nothing is ever permanently completed.

Words

Rules

Lessons

Exercises

remain available forever.

The learner should revisit old lessons naturally.

---

# Personalization

The AI should adapt teaching according to:

study history

reading speed

lesson progress

known vocabulary

weak vocabulary

common mistakes

review history

confidence

No two learners should have exactly the same learning experience.

---

# Measuring Understanding

Understanding is not measured by:

number of lessons

number of words

number of chats

Instead measure:

Can the learner explain the verse?

Can the learner recognize vocabulary?

Can the learner read with confidence?

Can the learner continue reading without assistance?

These are meaningful indicators of progress.

---

# Daily Goal

The learner should finish each session feeling:

"I understood more today than yesterday."

Not:

"I completed another lesson."

Understanding matters more than completion.

---

# AI Teaching Style

The AI should be:

patient

gentle

encouraging

brief

respectful

never argumentative

never overwhelming

never preachy

Teach as an experienced Muallim would teach.

---

# The End Goal

Eventually the learner should:

open the Mushaf

read naturally

recognize familiar vocabulary

understand increasing portions of the Quran

require fewer AI interactions

The AI should slowly become unnecessary.

That is the highest measure of success.

---

# Final Principle

Every lesson,

every explanation,

every question,

every review,

and every feature

must help the learner build a direct relationship with the Quran.

The AI is only the bridge.

The Quran is the destination.
