Iqra Quran Reader

Most people who grew up reciting the Quran can read the Arabic without stumbling — and understand almost none of it. Iqra is built for that gap. Tap any word and its Urdu meaning appears. Mark it as known, and it turns green every time it shows up across the whole Quran — so by the time you've read Al-Faatiha a few times, you start recognising الرَّحْمَٰنِ everywhere.

It won't make you fluent in a week. But if you use it while reading the Quran anyway, the words start sticking.

02

Home Screen

When you open the app, you see where you left off — which surah, which page — and a button to go straight back. The stats strip below shows today's pages against your goal, your streak, and your current level. If you've saved any words, there's a review card too.

Home
  1. 1Resume card — last surah and page. Tap Open mushaf to continue reading.
  2. 2Daily stats — pages today, day streak, current level.
  3. 3Daily review — words you've saved. Tap Review to run through them.
  4. 4Quick links — Words & Qawaid, Duas, My words, Progress.
03

Mushaf

Reads like a physical mushaf — one page at a time, same right-to-left layout. The difference is that every Arabic word is a button. Swipe left or right to turn pages, or use the Prev / Next buttons at the bottom.

Mushaf Screen
  1. 1Surah name & chevron — tap to jump to a different Surah, Juz, or page number.
  2. 2% of words on this page you've already marked as known.
  3. 3Toolbar — Search · Bookmark · Font size (Aa) · Page view toggle.
  4. 4Ayah number badge — tap it to show the full Urdu translation of that ayah.
  5. 5Bottom bar — Prev / Next, daily goal ring, streak flame.
All 604 pages are saved on your device after the first load. Airplane mode, no signal — doesn't matter.
04

Word Meanings

Tap any Arabic word — on a Mushaf page, inside a dua, in search results, anywhere — and a dark card slides up. It shows the word in large script, the Urdu meaning, and how many times that word appears across the whole Quran. That frequency number is worth paying attention to: once you know الرَّحْمَٰنِ, you've unlocked a word that shows up 169 times.

Meaning Card
  1. 1Tapped word is highlighted in the text above.
  2. 2Reference — which surah and ayah this word is from.
  3. 3Arabic word in large Scheherazade script.
  4. 4Urdu meaning in Nastaliq.
  5. 5I know this · +5 XP — marks the word known, turns it green globally.
  6. 6Full ayah translation — expands the complete Urdu translation.
  7. 7How many times this word appears in the Quran.
Tapped a word by mistake and marked it known? Tap it again — the button will say "Known word ✓" and tapping removes the status.
05

Search

The search ignores diacritics, so you don't need to type رَبِّ exactly — just رب works. Switch between Whole words and Any part depending on how well you remember the phrase. Results show up as Mushaf-style ayahs with the match highlighted in yellow and any words you already know highlighted in green.

Search Results
  1. 1Search bar — type Arabic or Urdu, diacritics optional.
  2. 2Whole words / Any part — changes whether partial matches count.
  3. 3Result count — shows the first 150 of however many were found.
  4. 4View all on one page — opens every matching ayah together, mushaf-style.
  5. 5Yellow = this word matched. Green = you've marked it known. Tap either for its meaning.
  6. 6Open › — jumps to that ayah's page in the Mushaf.

07

Duas

Two halves: Qur'anic duas drawn from specific ayahs (filtered by Juz), and Daily masnoon prophetic supplications. The word-tap works the same as in the Mushaf — so if you're memorising Ayat ul Kursi or the last three quls, you can tap each word while you learn it. Marking a dua as memorised earns 25 XP.

Duas Screen
  1. 1Qur'anic / Daily masnoon tabs.
  2. 2Juz filter — narrow down to duas from a specific part of the Quran.
  3. 3Dua title and Quran reference, e.g. Al-Faatiha 1:5–7 · Juz 1.
  4. 4Tappable Arabic text — tap any word for its meaning. Known words tinted green.
  5. 5Urdu translation in Nastaliq script.
  6. 6Mark memorised · +25 XP.
08

Words & Qawaid

Seven units following the Muallim-ul-Quran curriculum. Each unit has two tabs. Words lists the vocabulary for that unit — tap a word to see its meaning, tap the check to mark it known. Qawaid shows the grammar rules with real Quranic phrases as examples. The rules won't replace a grammar class, but seeing ال explained and then immediately tapping through six Quranic examples that use it is a faster way to make it stick than reading a definition alone.

Qawaid · Unit 1
  1. 1Words / Qawaid tab toggle.
  2. 2Progress bar — words known in this unit out of total.
  3. 3Rule number and Arabic term — e.g. RULE 1 · ال (the definite article).
  4. 4Urdu explanation of the rule.
  5. 5Tappable Quranic examples showing the rule in actual use.
  6. 6Find in Quran › — searches the whole Quran for that phrase.
09

Progress & XP

The Progress screen keeps score. Your level goes up as you earn XP — 5 per word you mark known, 25 per dua memorised. The bar chart shows pages read per day against your daily goal (the dashed line). The streak counter tracks consecutive days of reading; if you miss a day, a streak freeze covers it. You earn one freeze for every seven-day streak.

Progress Screen
  1. 1Level card — name (e.g. Reader), total XP, and how far to the next level.
  2. 2Weekly stats — pages, new words, and XP earned, compared to last week.
  3. 3Reading chart — each bar is one day; dashed line is your daily goal.
  4. 4Pages today ring — how close you are to today's target.
  5. 5Day streak and freeze count — freezes cover a missed day automatically.

Settings & Install

A few things worth adjusting when you first open the app: bump the font size up if the Arabic is hard to read on your screen, and set a daily goal you'll actually hit — 2 pages a day is more sustainable than 10 for most people starting out.

Settings (tap Aa in the Mushaf toolbar)

SettingOptions
Font sizeSmall / Medium / Large
Page viewFull page (like a mushaf) or continuous scroll
Word tintingOn = known words highlighted green while reading
Dark modeToggle in the More ≡ drawer
Daily goal2, 5, or 10 pages

Install on iPhone or iPad

Must be Safari — Chrome and Firefox on iOS can't install PWAs.

1
Tap the Share button (the box with an arrow) in the Safari toolbar.
2
Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. The app opens full-screen from now on, no browser bar.

Install on Android

1
Open in Chrome. Tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen, or tap the Install banner if one appears at the bottom.

Install on a computer

Look for the ⊕ install icon in the address bar of Chrome or Edge and click it.

Once installed, the app is fully offline. Your vocabulary, streak, and XP are saved on your device — nothing is sent anywhere.
