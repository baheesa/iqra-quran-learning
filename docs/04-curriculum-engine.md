# 04 - Curriculum Engine

> "The Curriculum Engine is the heart of the learning system.
> It decides what the learner studies, when they study it, and how knowledge is reinforced."

---

# Purpose

The Curriculum Engine transforms the Muallim-ul-Quran books from scanned PDFs into a structured learning journey.

It is responsible for preserving the teaching methodology exactly as intended by Muallim-ul-Quran.

The AI follows the Curriculum Engine.

The Curriculum Engine does not follow the AI.

---

# Why a Curriculum Engine?

Most AI applications work like this:

User asks

↓

AI answers

This project is different.

The learner follows a curriculum.

The AI supports that curriculum.

Learning should never become random.

---

# Source of Truth

The Curriculum Engine is built from:

Muallim-ul-Quran

Image PDFs

Nothing else defines lesson progression.

# Lesson Completion

A lesson is considered complete only when:

✓ Vocabulary reviewed

✓ Rules understood

✓ Exercises attempted

✓ Quran reading completed

✓ Daily reflection completed

Completion is based on understanding.

Not on clicking "Next."

# Final Principle

The Curriculum Engine protects the integrity of Muallim-ul-Quran.

It ensures that the AI never becomes the teacher.

The curriculum remains the teacher.

The AI simply delivers it in a personalized, patient, and consistent way.
